# Governance Architecture

## 1. Overview

This document defines the **Governance Architecture** for the Finanças Pessoais platform.

Its purpose is to establish a **FAANG-grade governance operating model** that ensures:

- Strategic alignment
- Security governance
- Technology governance
- Data governance
- Financial governance
- AI governance
- Compliance governance
- Operational governance
- Risk governance
- Audit governance
- Decision accountability
- Enterprise transparency

Governance architecture ensures that **technology, business, security, compliance, and operational decisions are made in a controlled, auditable, scalable, and resilient way**.

Governance aims to ensure:

- Business strategy alignment

## 13. Exception Governance

When policies cannot be followed:

Required:

- Formal exception request
- Risk assessment
- Time-bounded approval
- Compensating controls
- Executive review if critical
- Expiration tracking

Exception record includes:

- Policy affected
- Business reason
- Risk accepted
- Owner
- Expiration
- Controls

## 14. Governance Escalation Model

---

### Critical Governance Failures

Examples:

- Security policy bypass
- Compliance breach
- Financial integrity compromise
- Board-level risk

Escalation:

Immediate

---

### High Governance Issues

Examples:

- Repeated policy violations
- Control failure
- High-risk exception

Escalation:

< 24h

---

### Medium Issues

Escalation:

< 7 days

---

### Low Issues

Handled in normal governance cycle
